Your calls is where a review starts: a row for each decision the run left for you, and the way out at the foot. Its count is how many are left.

Check these yourself

First come the criteria Fabrika didn't test, because their test level can't run cleanly in this project. Each says how to check it by hand. Open the app if there is one, try it, and mark it works or doesn't. doesn't goes to the repair loop like any other call. See When a level isn't checked.

A call

Each row is an objection the run couldn't settle on its own, with any findings that say the same thing gathered under it. Open it to read the arbiter's account first, then each agent's own words, verbatim, the files and criteria it names, and what the repair loop already tried. Nothing on the row is paraphrased.

Write a note if you have one, then tag it:

  • repair: the repair loop makes the narrowest change that resolves it. Your note is the objective the repairer reads.
  • dismiss: no work for the repair loop. Your note is your reason, and it stays on the record.

Something no row covers goes in + Something none of these covers, in your words; it reaches the repair loop as written.

Where it goes

The card at the foot works out where the review goes from your tags; you don't pick it. Until every call is in, it says Waiting on your calls. One repair is enough to make it a repair round: Send to the repair loop resumes the build against the same spec, with your flags already routed. Until you send it, a tag on the card can still be changed, and the route works itself out again.

If everything was dismissed, there's nothing for the loop to do, and the card points you to the cord to rule on the packet as it stands. See Repairs and the repair loop and Ruling on the packet.
