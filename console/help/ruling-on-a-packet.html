Your ruling ends a feature: accept the branch as it stands, or send it back. It's made on the packet page, beside every finding and decision it accepts.

Getting there

Press Rule on this packet in the bar of any review tab, or follow the cord at the foot of Your calls once every call is in. The packet page is the same review read the long way round: the headline, who looked and what the loop did, the strongest objection raised, the decisions, every finding with what became of it, traceability, the checks, and what the workers disclosed in their own words.

Decisions

Workers record the choices they made: the rationale, the alternatives they rejected, how hard each is to undo, and how confident they were. The list is ranked by how badly each needs a person, and one that can't be undone, that's under half confident, or that touches security, data loss or scope is marked as needing you. Accept or Send back each one. A ruling is recorded, not applied: it says you saw the choice and let it stand, or didn't. You can re-rule later.

The cord

  • Release the line — accept, then Accept, with all of that standing: the branch is accepted as it stands, and any blockers, unrepaired findings and unbuilt requirements stay on the record with it. Fabrika commits the frozen spec onto the branch, removes the checkout and keeps the branch. Merging it is yours: Fabrika never merges.
  • Pull the cord — send it back, then Send it back: the feature is recorded rejected. The branch and its checkout are left standing, so you can look at what was built.

Each press shows its consequence before the second press acts, and neither can be taken back. To have Fabrika keep working on it instead, tag calls repair and send them to the loop before you rule: see Your calls. To measure or build it again, see Verify again, build again, resume, discard.
