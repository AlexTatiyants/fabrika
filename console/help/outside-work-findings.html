Every round of outside work is attacked the way a feature's review is, without a spec: probes written against it, a review panel and the arbiter. What they find is on the Findings tab, and you call each finding fix or accept.

The attack

  • Breaker: writes probes, which are tests aimed first at the claims no test shows yet, and runs them in the project's environment. They're written in the round's own worktree and never on the branch. A probe that fails today shows a defect, and passes once the defect is fixed.
  • Review: the same review agents a feature has, set on the crew page. They read the branch against its claims, with the checks and what the probes did.
  • Arbiter: routes every finding. A defect is sent back to whoever built the branch. What only a person can decide is your call. A tidy-up is cleanup. What isn't wrong is not a defect. Nothing is repaired during the attack.

A finding

Each row shows the finding's severity, its title and where the arbiter sent it. Findings that say the same thing are gathered under one row. Open a row to read the arbiter's account first, then each agent's own words, the probe that showed it and what it printed, and the files it names, linked into The code.

Your call

  • fix: it goes back, to be fixed by Fabrika or by whoever built the branch.
  • accept: a risk you sign for. Your note is your reason. It stays on the record and goes into the pull request.

A defect the arbiter sent back stays going back until you accept it. Calls can be changed until the round ends. Copy all as a prompt, or copy a prompt for this one on a row, hands what's going back to whoever will fix it.

Against the round before

From the second round on, each finding is marked against the round before. It's new, still there after a fix, accepted before, or code changed. Above the rows, Since round N lists what Fabrika fixed, and what it couldn't.

A risk you accepted carries forward: the same finding in the next round is accepted again, with your note, and says which round you accepted it in. The exception is when the files it names changed between the two rounds' heads. Then it's marked code changed, and it's your call again.

The tidy branch

When the attack found cleanup, or wrote probes worth keeping as tests, Fabrika can put them on a branch of its own, fabrika/tidy/<branch>. Press Make it, then take what you want from it. A probe that fails there keeps failing until the fix that it tests.

What to do with what's going back is in Rounds, fixes and the ending.
