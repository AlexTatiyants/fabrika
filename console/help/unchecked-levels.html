When a test level has no test runner, or its tests can't clean up after themselves, Fabrika doesn't check criteria at that level. You check them by hand when you review.

Why skip rather than try

Tests that can't run cleanly fail for reasons that have nothing to do with the code: another test's leftovers, a missing runner. A failure like that looks exactly like a broken feature, so it would cost you time and tell you nothing. Fabrika leaves those criteria to you instead.

Where you'll see it

  • The project page says how many levels are checked, and which aren't, at the top and on each level's row.
  • The spec says, above the criteria, how many won't be checked by Fabrika, and tags each one you check. Freezing the spec agrees to that.
  • Review starts with Check these yourself: each criterion with how to check it. Mark it works or doesn't. Doesn't becomes an ordinary call you can send for repair.
  • The verdict is never a plain Ships while criteria went untested. At best it's Ships, with rulings, and the headline says what's left for you to check.

What still runs

Everything else: your project's own checks, including any existing test suite at that level, and every criterion at the levels Fabrika can test.

Changing it

Set up the missing level (see When Fabrika asks you to change your repository) and re-survey. The next feature is checked at that level too.
