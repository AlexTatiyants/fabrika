Outside work is worked in rounds until you underwrite it or drop it. Sending a defect back doesn't end the work. It's how the next round starts.

Sending it back

While anything is going back, the foot of the Findings tab offers two ways to fix it:

  • Fix it: Fabrika fixes it, on fabrika/outside/<branch>. That branch is its own for this work, made from yours at the first fix and moved forward each round after. First it commits the probes, as Add tests from Fabrika's review of <branch>. They are the fence. Then the repairer fixes what's going back, with your note as its objective. Then every check that passed and every probe runs again:
    • A repair that breaks something that held before is thrown away.
    • A probe settles the finding it shows.
    • The agent that raised each other finding checks it again.
    Each fix is committed under what it fixes. It's bounded like a feature's loop, by rework.max_rounds and rework.max_attempts_per_finding. When it's done, the next round opens by itself, at the fixed head.
  • Copy the prompt: every defect going back, with your notes and the tests that show them, for whoever built the branch. Fix them on your branch, then press Look again.

Your branch is never written to, either way.

Your new commits

Once Fabrika has a branch for the work, a round looks at that branch. If you've committed to yours since, the next round merges your new commits into Fabrika's branch first, as Merge your commits from <branch>. If they conflict with its fixes, nothing is merged and the round doesn't start. It names the files instead: merge fabrika/outside/<branch> into your branch, settle them there, and look again. Fabrika's own commits are never read as claims. Only yours are.

The rounds

Once there's more than one round, a strip under the line shows each one: its head, on your branch or Fabrika's, and what came of it, with what Fabrika fixed between rounds. Select a round to read it as it was. The latest is shown by default.

The ending

  • Underwrite: you sign for the work at this round's head. Every finding has to be accepted first, and every call made. Until then, the box above the button says what's left. Fabrika opens a pull request where the project's settings say pull requests go. It's opened from Fabrika's branch if it fixed anything, and from yours if not, at the commit you underwrote. Its body holds:
    • what the branch is;
    • the checks that passed;
    • the claims a test shows;
    • every risk you accepted, with your reason;
    • what Fabrika fixed, round by round.
    With nowhere set, nothing leaves this machine. Merging it is yours: Fabrika never merges.
  • Drop it: the work is recorded dropped. Every branch stays where it is.

Neither can be taken back. A feature ends the same two ways: see Ruling on the packet.
