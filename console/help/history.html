Everything that has happened to your project, and who did it: you, or Fabrika.

What's on it

What Fabrika wrote into your repository, with each commit. The decisions you made: checks you accepted, changes you took or turned down, edits. Every time the checks ran, and what moved since the time before. Every reading of your repository, and what it cost. And each feature: when you started it, when it came back for review, and what you ruled.

The four numbers at the top are also filters. So are the buttons under them.

What's left out

Nothing is hidden, but some things are folded. Re-readings in a row, with nothing of yours between them, are one line carrying the latest one's proposal. Checks that failed in a feature and were re-run at the commit it started from, in the same minute, are one line. Fabrika's own timings aren't shown.

Each line ends with the number of the record it was read from; press it to see the record. Show every record, at the bottom, is the whole ledger as it was written, append-only.

A feature's own history

A feature has a step-by-step record of its own, every exchange with every agent, on its page. This one says only when it started and when it came back to you.
